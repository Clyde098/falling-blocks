How to Play

Keyboard

  • ← → Move left / right
  • ↓ Soft drop
  • ↑ Rotate clockwise
  • Z / Q Rotate counter-clockwise
  • X / E Rotate clockwise
  • Space Hard drop
  • C / Shift Hold piece
  • P / Esc Pause
  • R Restart

Mobile

  • Swipe left / right to move
  • Swipe down to soft drop
  • Swipe up to hard drop
  • Tap to rotate
  • On-screen buttons available

Scoring

  • Single: 100 × level
  • Double: 300 × level
  • Triple: 500 × level
  • Tetris (4 lines): 800 × level
  • T-Spin bonuses available
  • Combos and back-to-back multipliers
